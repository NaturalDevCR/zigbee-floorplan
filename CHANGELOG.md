# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Floor plan editor: grid with snapping, walls, rooms, measuring, undo/redo, autosave, import and export.
- Several floors per building and several buildings per site, with a whole-site view where buildings can be shown, hidden and positioned.
- Background images with a calibrated scale.
- Wall and slab materials with editable attenuation.
- Zigbee2MQTT integration: devices, availability, link quality, battery and on-demand network map scans.
- Mesh view with inferred routes to the coordinator, hop counts, parents and children, and flags for devices missing from the last scan.
- Coverage estimate with a multi-wall model, calibration from real link quality, weak device and link lists, and router suggestions.
- Sidebar panel and Lovelace card with a visual editor. Light and dark themes, English and Spanish.
